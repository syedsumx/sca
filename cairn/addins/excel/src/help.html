Cairn for Excel

Sign in from Home ▸ Cairn. Every answer comes back as you: Cairn's masks, row filters and table permissions apply as they do in Cairn. Results spill from the cell, headers first.

=CAIRN.QUERY(sql, [connection])

Runs a read-only query, e.g. =CAIRN.QUERY("SELECT region, sum(amount) FROM sales.orders GROUP BY 1").

=CAIRN.METRIC(name, [dimensions], [filters])

A governed metric, e.g. =CAIRN.METRIC("revenue", "region", "region = EU; amount >= 10"). Dimensions and filters may also be ranges of cells. Operators: = != > >= < <= in (region in EU, US).

=CAIRN.ASK(question)

Asks a question of your data, e.g. =CAIRN.ASK("orders per region last month").
